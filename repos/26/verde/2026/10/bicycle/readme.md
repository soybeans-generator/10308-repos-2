BIKE COMPARISON: Cannondale Quick 3  vs  Priority L-Train

FRAME
  Quick 3    SmartForm C3 Alloy
             (= 6061 aluminum, Cannondale's entry-tier frame)
  L-Train    Double-butted chromoly steel

FORK
  Quick 3    Steel
  L-Train    Not confirmed on the page I pulled

WEIGHT
  Quick 3    ~26 lb (estimate, not published)
  L-Train    27 lb (published; V-brake version)
             30 lb with disc brakes

TIRES
  Quick 3    35mm Schwalbe Spicer PLUS (puncture belt)
             slower rolling than a plain slick
  L-Train    ~35mm

RIDING POSITION
  Quick 3    Forward / fitness
  L-Train    Upright / commuter

DRIVETRAIN
  Quick 3    Derailleur, 8-speed, 40T x 11-45
  L-Train    Gates belt + Shimano Nexus 8 internal hub
             (costs about 3-5% in drivetrain efficiency)

GEARING
  Quick 3    Climb 24.2"  |  Top 98.9" = 26.5 mph @ 90 rpm
  L-Train    Climb 32.6"  |  Top ~26.5 mph @ 90 rpm (INFERRED)
             Priority does not publish sprocket sizes. The estimate
             assumes 50T x 22T, which matches the belt length and
             chainstay. If it is actually 46T x 24T, top gear falls
             to roughly 22 mph and the L-Train is the slower bike.

VERDICT
  Faster:  Quick 3, and comfortably so. Forward position and a
           derailleur are both worth more than the 1 lb gap.
           It also has the easier climbing gear (24.2" vs 32.6").
  Caveat:  The only figure that favours the L-Train is its top
           gear, and that number is my inference, not a spec.
           Discard it and nothing argues for the L-Train on speed.
